# Pulseframe refinements after Pulseframe_v1.1

The `Pulseframe_v1.1` tag preserves the source before these changes. Only Pulse Ring and Spectrum remain in the editor and renderer. Older project files migrate Halo, Tunnel and Bloom to Pulse Ring, and Wave and Aurora to Spectrum, retaining the selected style's logo layout and explicit wave-focus preference.

Pulse Ring defaults to **Full sound**, with **Bass waves** second and **Auto** as an optional third choice. Bass focus deliberately covers 35–450 Hz; this could leave a nearly resting contour in otherwise audible vocal or treble passages. Full sound represents the complete supported log-frequency range. Auto smoothly blends bass and full-range contours according to same-window linear band power; it also follows mixed passages with steady bass and changing treble. The quiet threshold, visible resting ring, causal kick window, stereo power handling, transparent trailing layers and source-time replay history remain in place. A constant tone can correctly produce a stable contour.

Spectrum now uses the same smoothed linear-amplitude stereo log bands as Pulse Ring, rather than sampling normalized byte magnitudes below a fixed FFT-bin ceiling. Each bar pools its frequency range so isolated narrow tones and sub-bass survive smaller bar counts. The mirrored bars, center line and gradient are centered at the exact canvas midpoint. Scale changes bar width and bounded height without shifting that midpoint. Glow margins protect all aspect ratios. Fitted logos, subtitles and track details have reserved space. Caption spacing measures the largest actual wrapped subtitle; bottom track details use a compact footer row when Spectrum has captions, preserving room for the bars.

Beat lighting offers **Rays**, **Aura**, **Spotlights** and **Edge glow**, plus an intensity slider. Quick Looks include their lighting settings. Controls use at least 44px touch targets. The lightweight Canvas effects use source time and warmed audio response for preview and export, and are suppressed under reduced-motion preferences.

No adjacent audible samples had mean contour change below .0001. These are renderer response checks, not a claim of perceptual or device-latency accuracy.

Native Canvas rendering and automated geometry checks were available; browser touch gestures and device video export were not re-tested in this pass.
